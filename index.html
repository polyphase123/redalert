    Strategic Recommendations for Grid Operators & Regulators
    Core policy and market monitoring updates derived from the May 2026 grid reliability failures
    1 Conglomerate Market Power Assessment

    ERC and PEMC should evaluate market power at the Conglomerate parent group level across different asset classes, rather than isolated corporate entities. A group's base-load outage should not lead to unmitigated pricing windfalls on its own affiliate peaking or battery storage merchant assets during grid alerts.

    2 Review of BESS Market Pricing Rules

    Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are repeatedly setting WESM prices at the ₱32,000/MWh ceiling despite having negligible marginal fuel costs. A revised pricing mechanism is critical for energy storage resources under ancillary contracts to prevent artificial pricing spikes.

    3 Strict Enforcement of ERC Resolution 04

    Generation companies with units exceeding annual unplanned caps (like EERI at +75 days) must face systematic regulatory audits and financial penalties, ensuring that the financial burden of purchasing spot replacement power is not passed down to end consumers.

    Why is Manual Load Dropping Implemented?

    Manual Load Dropping (MLD) or rotational brownouts are executed as a last resort measure when the available electricity supply drops far below the consumer demand.

    By cutting off power temporarily in controlled blocks (rotational schedules), NGCP prevents a total, catastrophic collapse of the electrical grid (blackout), which would take days to restore.

    Barring unexpected plant trips, MLD schedules may be shortened or cancelled if the actual system demand drops below the projected peak.
